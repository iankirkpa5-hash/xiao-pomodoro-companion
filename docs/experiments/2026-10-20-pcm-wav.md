## Listening And Operator Observations

1. First raw WAV: the operator reported noise/interruption, then specified pops
   or crackling. Transfer PASS was not treated as audio-quality PASS.
2. Kept the raw WAV unchanged and generated two labeled listening comparisons:
   A removes whole-record DC; B additionally applies an 80 Hz high-pass filter.
   Both use a shared bounded fixed gain and 10 ms endpoint fades.
3. Operator preferred A and reported blowing while pronouncing words. Direct
   airflow is a plausible contributor, not a proven sole cause of the noise.
4. Retest prompt: microphone to the side of the mouth, about 20-30 cm away,
   normal voice, no direct breath. The operator confirmed readiness; exact
   distance was not measured.
5. After listening to the retest's centered preview, the operator reported
   "low volume, but clear content." Treat basic capture/playback as usable;
   comfortable playback loudness remains an improvement item. This is not a
   formal signal-to-noise, latency, or lossless-recording certification.
6. Operator confirmed screen, touch Start/Pause, Settings, and Bright are all
   normal on this firmware. Reset/Stats/power-cycle persistence and a full Focus
   completion were not repeated in this session.

No microphone threshold or I2S-format change was made in response to the first
noisy sample. No AI cleanup, silence gating, automatic gain, or fabricated
samples were used. Original reports retain their generated pending-manual
fields; this document records the subsequent operator feedback separately.

## Boundary And Next Step

The achieved step is **bounded raw PCM capture -> verified local WAV -> operator
listening**, while preserving the tested Pomodoro interactions. Files stay local
and outside Git by default.

Keep the original WAV as evidence. Playback gain can be tuned separately from
capture and Quiet/Normal/Loud thresholds. DMA overrun event counting, repeated
recording stress tests, extended recordings, FFT, STT, and AI are not part of this
acceptance. See the limitations in the [recorder guide](../pcm-recording.md).

Follow-up: the [host preview gain record](2026-10-22-pcm-playback-gain.md)
raised the default preview gain to 16x after listening; raw capture and device
thresholds were unchanged.
